# Acceptance repairs before development runs

Two concrete patches passed the original independent acceptance on preparation
head `f01ac872987d822e718ce4ef61e7ea43326e6f8d` while violating the public task.
They are now rejected by completed independent checks. The eight-task set,
direct/D configurations, native product, sixteen future assignments and
600-second total task budget are unchanged. Real provider calls and real
development task-runs are both zero.

## Actual counterexamples before and after

The original judge was exercised before any acceptance edit, using the existing
`controlPatch`, `evaluatePatch` and node:test reporter in an offline container.
Neither result is a constructed scorer input. The complete additional patches
are retained and regenerated byte-for-byte in the ordinary preflight.

| Complete negative patch | R on original judge | R on corrected judge | New rejection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Discount field loss](../tasks/01-invoice-discount/discount-field-loss.patch) | true | false | `fd01.discount-roundtrip` |
| [Refund all holds](../tasks/03-wallet-cancel/all-holds-refund.patch) | true | false | `fd03.cancel-one-hold-accounting` |

The invoice patch starts from gold and deletes `discountPercent` from calculated
items before returning the bill. Totals and validation remain correct, but both
invoice items and exported lines lose the field. The original independent judge
completed three PASS obligations and exited 0. The unchanged public test exited
1 because exported `discountPercent` was absent instead of 20.

The wallet patch starts from gold and refunds the sum of all current holds while
deleting only the selected id. Starting at 200, reserving a=50 and b=70 produced
available=80 and both holds. Cancelling a persisted available=200, holds={b:70}
and settled=[]. The original independent judge completed three PASS obligations
and exited 0; the existing public tests also passed.

With the corrected judge, both full patches apply and exit 1 with no launch
error or signal. Invoice completes three obligations, with one FAIL; wallet
completes four obligations, with one FAIL. There are no cancelled, skipped or
todo tests. Both return `status=FAIL`, `R=false`, with verified cleanup.
See the separate [original-head receipt](acceptance-controls-before.json) and
[corrected-content receipt](acceptance-fix.json); raw process output remains
local and is not committed.

## Independent expectations and obligation split

`fd01.discount-roundtrip` specifies all four expected line objects directly:
fractional 12.5% discount with total 265, 100% with total 0, explicit zero with
total 50 and an omitted discount with total 38. The known total is 353. Both
invoice items and exported lines must independently equal those objects in
order, including original fields, own-field presence/omission and unchanged
inputs. No tested production helper computes the expectations.

`fd03.cancel-one-hold-accounting` is registered under **feature** in task.json.
It checks the complete persisted sequence 200 -> 80 -> 130, retaining only
b=70 after cancellation, a false repeat cancellation without a write, and a
commit of b with available still 130 and settled=['b']. Accounting totals are
checked against the known 200 before/after cancellation and after the known
70-cent committed debit. Expectations do not use actual available balance.
The existing `fd03.other-holds-preserved` and `fd03.deposit-and-rejection`
preservation tests and their registration are unchanged; baseline passes both.

A single bounded read of the other six acceptance files looked only for actual
output used as the sole expected value for an explicit public contract. No
additional concrete case was found or changed. This is not a general audit of
all possible edge cases.
