# Subscribe repeatability: both new ON runs deliver and repair the observed defect

The predeclared engineering threshold is met on this known development task.
Both new ON runs have Q=true, T=true and D=true; both OFF runs have Q=false,
T=true and D=false. In each ON, a real compiler counterexample reached the next
request and the author immediately repaired the introduced old-API narrowing,
retaining the complete subscribe feature. No external interruption explains the
delivery difference.

This supports proposing a separate transfer stage. It does not authorize or
start that stage, establish general lift, compare against ordinary OpenCode, or
justify a runtime/default change. TYPE_COMPAT remains experimental/default-off.
These are repeated executions of one previously used EventEmitter3 task, not
held-out projects or six independent tasks. No pooled effectiveness percentage
or transfer interval is calculated.
