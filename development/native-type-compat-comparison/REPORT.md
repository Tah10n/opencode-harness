# Four-run TYPE_COMPAT development result

Keep TYPE_COMPAT experimental and default-off, without scheduling another campaign. The frozen batch does not establish an additional complete delivery caused by compatibility diagnostics. ON delivered B, but OFF's correct B patch lost normal delivery to a provider overload; ON detected no incompatibility and caused no repair. A failed in both configurations before any diagnostic ran.

This compares TYPE_COMPAT with the same direct workflow, not with plain OpenCode. Four dependent development runs cannot establish stable general lift. A is the known EventEmitter3 subscribe development example. B is a previously unmeasured waitFor/AbortSignal extension in the same repository; cross-project transfer was not measured.

## Product decision

Retain the component as experimental, default off, with no automatically scheduled follow-up. Q ties on both tasks: both A patches fail and both B patches pass. Observed D is ON 1/2 versus OFF 0/2, but the B difference is confounded by provider interruption and lacks the required compiler-evidence-to-repair chain. No partial repair benefit was observed either. These runs do not justify promotion, a claim of transfer, superiority over plain OpenCode, or a new campaign without separate authorization.
