# A/B development result: benefit remains unproven

A and B work in the installed native direct workflow, but this stage did **not**
establish the requested practical quality improvement. Keep both experimental
flags off on the recommended path. The primary full-patch result is
**P 3/6, H00 6/6, H10 5/6, H01 6/6, H11 6/6**. H11 improves over plain OpenCode in
this sample, but matches direct without either addition. The small transfer
series stopped at an unknown provider submission and cannot confirm transfer.
No further model campaign, default promotion, merge or release was performed.

## What was delivered and what the evidence supports

| Layer | A: computed context | B: early executable checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implementation | Pinned TypeScript compiler resolves bounded static JS/TS links with locations, exports/re-exports, references, reverse import chains, tests/scripts and explicit limits; source changes refresh task-local context | Short native Bash interface selects declared project scripts, runs through ordinary permissions/cancellation and returns command, exit, diagnostics and limits; unchanged repeats are labelled historical |
| Installed connection | Ordinary read/literal-symbol grep feeds the query; all four independent flag settings verified in installed OpenCode | Same-session feedback and subsequent native tool calls verified with real commands, including prepared failure, correction and timeout |
| Luna received output | Primary 102 A replies; transfer 29 | Primary 67 replies: 22 real executions, 44 historical, one unsupported/not-run; transfer 7 replies: 3 real executions and 4 historical |
| Observed use | Related source/tests inspected, but many paths were already known through ordinary reads/search; no unique A discovery followed by a substantive B failure and repair was established | Primary lint feedback immediately led to repairs in H01 limitFunction, H01 listenerCount and H11 abortable waiters. Transfer B executions were all baseline passes before edits; later meaningful failures and repairs used ordinary Bash |
| Quality change | H10-H00 = -1/6; H11-H01 = 0 | H01-H00 = 0; H11-H10 = +1/6, driven by H10's failed deadline patch; no incremental H11 benefit over H00 |

The observed factorial interaction is +1/6, entirely from the clear-reason H10
failure. Six tasks do not establish synergy. B has demonstrated usable feedback;
A has demonstrated factual context delivery. Neither fact proves an improvement
in complete changes. Repeated historical replies also consumed context without
new execution, and parent reports sometimes reproduced long printf commands.
Runtime is materialized without research/evaluator imports. Disabled components
perform no hidden work; their actual outputs and costs are zero in disabled arms.

One diagnostic defect was found after the frozen primary series: words such as
`timeout` or `MODULE_NOT_FOUND` inside test names could misclassify B results.
It was corrected and verified with installed, model-free failing/passing/timeout
commands. The primary candidate stayed unchanged; transfer pinned the corrected
candidate separately. No primary B outcome carried the affected classification.
The common command observer still labels these npm/AVA/tsx workflows incomplete,
even when it lists actual exit-0 checks. This is a disclosed reporting limitation,
not an independent verdict on a patch and not a reason to alter the frozen basis.

## Transfer outcome and stopping boundary

H11 qualified under the rule fixed before primary outcomes: positive full-patch
balance against P, observed useful B feedback, no known serious unresolved
candidate regression. H01 tied it at 6/6; lower median full slot time selected
H11. H00 also scored 6/6, so selection never established incremental A/B benefit.
Two new public projects and tasks, dependencies, rubric, corrected runtime and
8-slot order were [frozen before transfer](../transfer/FREEZE.json).

The completed p-debounce repetitions tie at **1/2 each**, with one win each.
They are repetitions of one task, not two independent task successes. The
started-slot tally is P 1/3 and H11 2/3, but its extra H11 win is against an
operationally interrupted empty P capture. It cannot establish semantic quality
transfer. Both planned second p-throttle attempts remain not-started, not failures,
passes or zero-cost observed model outcomes.

Transfer slot 6 received five provider requests. Its fifth stream returned HTTP
200 and remained `in_progress` until `AbortError` about 180 seconds after forwarding.
The pre-existing relay/request stream timeout was 180 seconds; the 900-second
whole-task deadline was not reached. The scheduler reported `unknown_submission`,
stopped local work (exit 137), retained the empty patch and paused all new slots.
There was no quota refusal. Capture, local termination, forwarding shutdown and
relay removal were verified; active local provider handlers are zero. Server-side
completion and usage of that request remain unknown. No retry, replacement,
account/endpoint change or continuation was attempted. This operational limit
was inherited from the prior utility/Luna scheduler, not added for one arm.

All six captured states were applied as literal patches in ordinary project
copies. All six original project workflows passed; independent runtime/types
passed on the five nonempty patches. The unchanged empty p-throttle capture
failed the receiver requirements, so its type phase was not reached. The first
broad grading outcomes are retained; no post-outcome timing exception or broad
rerun was needed. Model-session timing/lint failures remain in execution evidence.

Full manual diff review found two additional **required regression coverage gaps**:

- H11 p-debounce repetition 1 submits only one replacement call after a real
  clear. It cannot assert that the latest receiver/arguments win over an earlier
  replacement. A bounded post-outcome control that retains the first replacement
  passes its delivered tests, while the frozen independent tests fail.
- P p-debounce repetition 2 tests active rejection only in the default no-op
  mode (`clearQueue()` returns 0). Real clearing cases have successful active
  work. A control swallowing an active error after a real clear passes its
  delivered clearQueue tests, while the frozen independent test fails.

Both original implementations pass independent behavior/types. These S=0 grades
are incomplete required test delivery, not invented runtime defects. A P
repetition-1 positive comparator's delivered tests fail each same control on the
specific intended assertion. [Control diffs and observed exits](transfer-sensitivity.json)
are supplied against the unchanged delivered patches. They diagnose requirements
already explicit in the frozen TASK; no new acceptance criterion, model retry or
author patch repair was introduced. Manual transfer review was not blind: identity
was inferable from the serial completion order. Earlier provisional notes and
final assessments are kept distinguishable in the grade records.

Native handoff and claims remain separate. Five transfer runs completed natively;
all three H11 parent reports conservatively said `incomplete`. One P final claims
completed delivery despite its test gap. The incomplete H11 report also quotes
an author summary omitting its test gap; that is unsupported author coverage,
not a false top-level complete status. No invented passing project command was
found. The interrupted P slot has no final answer; its initial planning text is
not counted as a final claim.

## Validation, reproducibility and cost

All authors used OpenCode 1.18.26, openai/gpt-5.6-luna/high, one shared 900-second
task deadline, the same per-task input and dependency snapshots, fresh sessions
and copies. Primary: 6 tasks across p-limit, Emittery and p-queue, 30 runs with
balanced five-arm order. Transfer: p-debounce 5.1.1 at `792115ea`, p-throttle
8.1.1 at `09f6e950`, 6 of 8 planned runs. Exact commits/digests are in the two
freezes. [Exact installed dependency inventories](dependencies/README.md) supplement
the source snapshots. Hidden evaluator/reference code was never in model inputs.
No full project copies, credentials or raw sessions are committed.

Pre-transfer model-free calibration covered 13 baseline/reference/alternative/
wrong-variant cases. All four final reference/alternative independent overlays
passed. Three formatted control project workflows passed; p-throttle alternative's
first broad run failed two existing callback-free strict timing assertions. One
focused baseline/alternative comparison passed both; the uniform treatment of
these specific timing cases was fixed before transfer. This is not reported as a
passing broad alternative run. [Preparation incidents](transfer-preparation-incidents.json)
retain sandbox refusals, an incorrect XO CLI path and control formatting errors.
The [primary report](PRIMARY.md) similarly discloses evaluator blind spots and
invalid supplementary setup attempts. No failed check is silently converted to pass.

Component times are already inside task times; do not add them again. Cached and
reasoning token fields are subsets, not extra usage. Sum of process times is not
CPU time or campaign wall time and can overlap. Preparation includes dependency
installation, fixtures, calibration and failed controls; it is not free. Some
fixture/preparation-only errors have no separate elapsed measurement. Real dollar
charges, server usage for interrupted requests, CPU/electricity cost and complete
per-operation orchestration billing are unavailable. The Codex goal counter is
separate from author-model usage and is reported as orchestration, not added tokens.
The [pre-publication counter snapshot](orchestration-cost.json) records 2,577,054
Codex goal tokens and 22,795 seconds; later publication adds overhead. This
includes orchestration/context work and must not be represented as Luna task cost.
Full request/token/component/slot breakdowns remain in the linked JSON files.

The stage leaves working opt-in tools and reproducible positive/negative evidence.
The hypothesis that A adds uniquely useful discovery, then B turns that discovery
into better complete patches did not receive support. B's primary repair feedback
is useful execution evidence, but neither the factorial result nor the incomplete
transfer supports promoting it or A+B as a quality improvement. No confirmatory
sample-size proposal is justified by a transfer signal that was not established;
no new campaign has been launched.
