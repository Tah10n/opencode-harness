## Mechanism observations

### Compact rules

The overall result was exactly neutral. Small tasks improved from 9/12 to
12/12, but five new HIGH/MEDIUM regressions exactly matched five resolved
defects overall. Median and mean duration ratios were 1.04 and 1.07. The
component is rejected and is not a basis for a default profile.

### Host-owned verification

The isolated P6 gate activated on all 28 eligible mutated tasks. It produced no
new critical regression and no timeouts, with median and mean duration ratios
of 0.79 and 0.95. Quality nevertheless declined from 69.44% to 66.67%; small
tasks declined from 12/12 to 11/12; five new HIGH/MEDIUM regressions exceeded
four resolved defects. Minimum-effect, positive-CI, exact-test, safety,
HIGH/MEDIUM, and small-task guardrails failed. P6 is development-only and
cannot advance to validation.

### Bounded repository map

The map activated on all 12 eligible medium tasks. Mean relevant-file recall,
consumer recall, and first-edit coverage were 1.00; mean context precision was
0.729; mean and maximum injected bytes were 1,198.25 and 1,327. Despite this,
medium regression-free success declined from 12/12 to 11/12, with one new
medium hidden-contract regression and no resolved defect. The component is
rejected.

### Automatic reviewer

The historical reviewer started on all 24 eligible medium/high tasks but only
15 returned an accepted closed result. Nine were `review_invalid_or_mutating`;
accepted reviews produced zero findings and zero reviewer-caused fixes. The
component therefore failed its >=95% operational activation gate independently
of its +2.78 pp point estimate.

Source `eb8525dfdc9033cd9acf31089f9f7a4f49ddad86` evaluated the repaired P7
reviewer after a successful model-backed acceptance smoke. In the full frozen
development campaign, 24 tasks required review, 19 reached verified integration
and started the reviewer, but zero reviews completed. All 19 starts settled as
`review_invalid_or_mutating`; the other five did not mutate and remained
`awaiting_verified_integration`. The candidate produced no accepted finding or
reviewer-caused fix. Regression-free success declined from 27/36 to 26/36:

## Reproduction

Run from a clean checkout of the source SHA in the documented Linux cgroup-v2
environment. Substitute the transition and a new development generation ID;
do not reuse a validation ordinal or any held-out selection:

```sh
npm run bench:v2:campaign -- \
  --split development \
  --generation <new-development-generation> \
  --baseline <baseline-arm> \
  --candidate <candidate-arm> \
  --model openai/gpt-5.6-luna \
  --provider openai \
  --variant low \
  --timeout-ms 300000 \
  --seed dev-small-boundary-search \
  --repetitions 1

npm run bench:v2:campaign:validate -- \
  --report .oc_harness/benchmark-v2/<development-report>.json
```

Validation, freeze, holdout selection, confirmatory campaigns, robustness, and
the real-repository pilot remain forbidden until a candidate passes the full
development gate and the subsequent sealed validation gate.
