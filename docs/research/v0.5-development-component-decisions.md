# v0.5 Development Component Decisions

Status: development evidence only. None of the results below is validation,
held-out, confirmatory, or product evidence. The persisted report fingerprints
bind the exact source, evaluator, policy, fixtures, model, provider, variant,
timeout, seed, ordering policy, paired outcomes, and summary. Full privacy-safe
envelopes currently exist outside Git and are not release artifacts; durable
artifact publication remains pending. Prompts, hidden files, reference
solutions, credentials, raw model output, and private absolute paths are not
published.

All campaigns used `openai/gpt-5.6-luna`, provider `openai`, variant `low`, a
300,000 ms attempt timeout, seed `dev-small-boundary-search`, one repetition,
and the 36-family development corpus (12 small, 12 medium, 12 high-risk).
Every report completed all 36 pairs and passed the canonical report validator.

## Mechanism observations

### Compact rules

The overall result was exactly neutral. Small tasks improved from 9/12 to
12/12, but five new HIGH/MEDIUM regressions exactly matched five resolved
defects overall. Median and mean duration ratios were 1.04 and 1.07. The
component is rejected and is not a basis for a default profile.

### Host-owned verification

The isolated P6 gate activated on all 28 eligible mutated tasks. It produced no
new critical regression and no timeouts, with median and mean duration ratios
of 0.79 and 0.95. Quality nevertheless declined from 69.44% to 66.67%; small
tasks declined from 12/12 to 11/12; five new HIGH/MEDIUM regressions exceeded
four resolved defects. Minimum-effect, positive-CI, exact-test, safety,
HIGH/MEDIUM, and small-task guardrails failed. P6 is development-only and
cannot advance to validation.

### Bounded repository map

The map activated on all 12 eligible medium tasks. Mean relevant-file recall,
consumer recall, and first-edit coverage were 1.00; mean context precision was
0.729; mean and maximum injected bytes were 1,198.25 and 1,327. Despite this,
medium regression-free success declined from 12/12 to 11/12, with one new
medium hidden-contract regression and no resolved defect. The component is
rejected.

### Automatic reviewer

The historical reviewer started on all 24 eligible medium/high tasks but only
15 returned an accepted closed result. Nine were `review_invalid_or_mutating`;
accepted reviews produced zero findings and zero reviewer-caused fixes. The
component therefore failed its >=95% operational activation gate independently
of its +2.78 pp point estimate.

Source `eb8525dfdc9033cd9acf31089f9f7a4f49ddad86` evaluated the repaired P7
reviewer after a successful model-backed acceptance smoke. In the full frozen
development campaign, 24 tasks required review, 19 reached verified integration
and started the reviewer, but zero reviews completed. All 19 starts settled as
`review_invalid_or_mutating`; the other five did not mutate and remained
`awaiting_verified_integration`. The candidate produced no accepted finding or
reviewer-caused fix. Regression-free success declined from 27/36 to 26/36:
small stayed 11/12, medium stayed 10/12, and high-risk declined from 6/12 to
5/12. Two new HIGH/MEDIUM regressions exceeded one resolved defect. Median and
mean duration ratios were 1.35 and 1.48; total tool calls rose from 368 to 576
and model turns from 36 to 60.

A bounded diagnostic replay reproduced the operational failure without reading
hidden or reference content. The reviewer returned the closed schema, left the
workspace unchanged, and had a complete stream with no mutation, dangerous,
network, unfinished, or rejected-path event. It was rejected solely because
one permitted shell invocation was classified as ambiguous. The reviewer
profile exposed a bounded read-only shell surface even though native read,
glob, and grep tools cover the required repository inspection. P7 is rejected
and cannot advance to composite or validation. A future development generation
may remove that shell surface, but it requires new acceptance and full
P6-to-P8 evidence; this report cannot be rerun or reinterpreted.

Source `bc82fe0ce1893c0f6b5eddf6f543e70a1d4abf71` removed reviewer shell access
and passed a model-backed acceptance with one required, started, and completed
review. The subsequent full P6-to-P8 campaign completed 14/24 required reviews;
19 started and five remained invalid, while five tasks never reached verified
integration. The 14 accepted reviews again produced zero findings and zero
reviewer-caused fixes. Regression-free success rose from 24/36 to 26/36, but
the 95% CI crossed zero, the exact paired test was not significant, and three
new HIGH/MEDIUM regressions remained despite five resolved defects. Small rose
from 9/12 to 10/12, medium declined from 11/12 to 10/12, and high-risk rose from
4/12 to 6/12. Median and mean duration ratios were 1.17 and 1.33; total tool
calls rose from 352 to 526 and model turns from 36 to 59.

Because no accepted review caused a finding or fix, the observed point estimate
cannot be attributed to the reviewer rather than stochastic differences in the
otherwise identical primary trajectories. The automatic reviewer is rejected
as a production component. P8 cannot advance to composite or validation, and
another reviewer-only generation is not justified by this evidence.

### Verification-failure remediation

Source `688e75969c31f9a7c67154b7846f46a1c2b8e871` evaluated one
host-triggered remediation retry after a failed trusted visible check. All six
eligible retries started and completed. Four changed the workspace and were
reverified; three then passed the same trusted check, one still failed, and two
completed without a change. This proves the bounded retry lifecycle executes,
but not that it improves task quality.

Regression-free success declined from 27/36 to 26/36. Small declined from
12/12 to 11/12; medium remained 10/12; high-risk remained 5/12. Three new
HIGH/MEDIUM regressions exceeded two resolved defects, while safety failures
increased from 9/36 to 10/36. The one-sided paired permutation p-value was
0.8125 and the 95% confidence interval was [-13.89, 8.33] pp. Median and mean
duration ratios were 1.21 and 1.03; tool calls rose from 379 to 393 and model
turns from 36 to 42. The activation and cost gates passed, but minimum effect,
positive CI, exact-test, HIGH/MEDIUM, safety, and small-task gates failed.

P9 is rejected and cannot advance to composite or validation. Aggregate retry
outcomes identify a bounded development direction: the host reports only that
verification failed, while the retry must rediscover the visible failure from
public files. A later architecture generation may provide a privacy-safe,
bounded diagnostic from the visible check, but must not expose hidden output,
the trusted executable, argv, check ID, or reference content. It requires new
acceptance and full development evidence; this report cannot be rerun or
reinterpreted.

The next development-only P10 generation keeps the same single-retry lifecycle
but supplies a host-built, 10,000-byte-bounded snapshot of the current public
diff. The snapshot is confined to allowed public paths changed by the first
attempt; it contains no check output or hidden/reference content. The retry is
explicitly required to inspect that diff and relevant public call sites or
tests before returning no change. P10 must pass new model-backed acceptance and
the full P6-to-P10 development gate before any plain composite or sealed
validation is allowed.

## Reproduction

Run from a clean checkout of the source SHA in the documented Linux cgroup-v2
environment. Substitute the transition and a new development generation ID;
do not reuse a validation ordinal or any held-out selection:

```sh
npm run bench:v2:campaign -- \
  --split development \
  --generation <new-development-generation> \
  --baseline <baseline-arm> \
  --candidate <candidate-arm> \
  --model openai/gpt-5.6-luna \
  --provider openai \
  --variant low \
  --timeout-ms 300000 \
  --seed dev-small-boundary-search \
  --repetitions 1

npm run bench:v2:campaign:validate -- \
  --report .oc_harness/benchmark-v2/<development-report>.json
```

Validation, freeze, holdout selection, confirmatory campaigns, robustness, and
the real-repository pilot remain forbidden until a candidate passes the full
development gate and the subsequent sealed validation gate.
