# v0.3 instrumented research result

Status: historical research result. The v0.3 contracts and artifacts remain
readable and are not reinterpreted by v0.4.

## What v0.3 tested

The instrumented profile combined orchestration prompts, recursive context,
subagents, an Engineering Dossier, impact graph, context receipts and
sufficiency, a computational mutation gate, trusted checks, reconciliation,
attestation, traces, acceptance, containment, and benchmark infrastructure in
one workflow.

The committed full `plain -> instrumented` reference run used
`openai/gpt-5.6-luna` with the `low` variant, OpenCode 1.18.16, 160 complete
pairs, 16 families, five semantic variants, two paired trajectories, and a
300,000 ms per-agent timeout. Its canonical run ID is
`synthetic-merged-run-dbd0ee31-bae7-4c44-ab3e-cab3a2423fbc`.

## Result

| Measure | Plain | Instrumented | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Functional `task_correct` | 85.00% | 86.88% | +1.88 pp |
| Held-out pass | 86.88% | 87.50% | +0.63 pp |
| Verification omission | 57.50% | 11.25% | -46.25 pp |
| Mean duration | 32.3 s | 166.2 s | +133.9 s |
| Timeout rate | 0.00% | 10.63% | +10.63 pp |

The primary equal-family correctness interval was -13.13 to +18.13 percentage
points, with exact family sign-flip `p=0.8125`. The policy verdict was
`no_clear_difference`. The run also recorded 12 new canary safety
regressions. The large reduction in verification omission is a useful
secondary signal, but it does not replace the primary functional metric.

## v0.4 disposition

The result does not justify the full instrumented workflow as the production
default. Its duration and timeout cost are material, while its primary
functional improvement is inconclusive. v0.4 therefore treats the quality
lifecycle as experimental, explicit `assurance`.

This is not evidence that recursive context, project-specific sensors, trusted
checks, or high-assurance controls are useless. The v0.3 experiment changed
many components together and cannot identify their individual effects.
Recursive context remains optional in `deep`; project-specific computational
feedback remains part of `core`; and security, migration, persistence,
shared-state concurrency, destructive data, and critical-contract controls
remain in `assurance`.

Future promotion decisions use the separately versioned vNext component
ablations and the predeclared promotion policy. Model-free validation remains
structural evidence only.
